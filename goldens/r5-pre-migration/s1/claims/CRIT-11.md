## Statement

**Proposition 4.1.** (a) Every $G$-orbital lies in one $D_\delta$; hence $\mathrm{rank}\,G\ge|A|+[n>|A|]$
with equality iff (2T). (b) $R^{\mathrm{pow}}$ meets $D_\delta$ for every $\delta\ne0$: for any
representative $v=m(q,p)$ of $\delta$ and any $i$, $(i,u^mi)\in D_\delta$ with $u$ the Christoffel
value of direction $(q,p)$. (c) $R^{\mathrm{pow}}$ meets $D_0$ iff (D0). Therefore
$$\text{(Q2)}\iff\text{every }G\text{-orbital inside every }D_\delta\text{ is met by some }u^m\text{ with }m(q,p)\in\delta.$$
(d) Under (EP): $u^m$ of direction $(q,p)$ meets only the $\iota$-class $m(q,p)\bmod2$;
**(EP) and $n>4$ ⇒ (Q1) fails** (single values have $m=1$ and miss $\{\iota=0\}\ne\emptyset$) —
the parity obstruction of S1 §3.2 in invariant form. Under (A3), $\iota$ is $G^+$-invariant.
