## Statement

> **Remark 5.6 (Jordan).** A finite group is never the union of the conjugates of
> a single proper subgroup. In the normal case the pairs reachable using one
> direction $u$ are the differences in $\bigcup_\gamma\langle u\rangle^\gamma$, so
> **a single direction never suffices unless it generates $G$** — an independent
> proof of Proposition 5.4, and the reason a "greedy" single-direction strategy is
> impossible outside the cyclic case.
