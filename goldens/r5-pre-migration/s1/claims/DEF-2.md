## Statement

**Definition 1.1.** $\Gamma:=(\mathbb Z/2)^{*4}=\langle r_L,r_R,r_B,r_T\rangle$. A *reflection
datum* is a finite transitive $\Gamma$-set $S$, the generators acting as involutions
$X_L,X_R,Y_B,Y_T$, such that some $X$ and some $Y$ have a fixed point. $m:=|S|$;
$H^+:=\langle X_L,X_R,Y_B,Y_T\rangle\le\mathrm{Sym}(S)$; $H:=$ image of
$\ker(\Gamma\to(\mathbb Z/2)^2)$ ($r_L,r_R\mapsto(1,0)$, $r_B,r_T\mapsto(0,1)$), a normal
subgroup of $H^+$ of index dividing 4, generated by the images of the five Schreier
generators $r_Lr_R,\ r_Br_T,\ r_Rr_Br_Rr_T,\ r_Rr_Tr_Rr_T,\ r_Tr_Lr_Tr_R$ (transversal $1,r_R,r_T,r_Tr_R$).

For a *corner type* $XY\in\{L,R\}\times\{B,T\}$ the orbits of the dihedral group $\langle X,Y\rangle$
on $S$ are *paths* (each involution has a fixed point on the orbit; length $\ell\ge1$) or
*cycles* (both act freely; even length $2\ell$, $\rho_{XY}:=XY$ of order $\ell$ there).
*Corner rotation*: $\rho_{XY}=XY$.

**Twisted diagonal.** $\Omega:=S\times(\mathbb Z/2)^2$ and
$$\sigma(s,e)=\big(X_{e_1}s,\ e+(1,0)\big),\quad \tau(s,e)=\big(Y_{e_2}s,\ e+(0,1)\big),\quad
\mu(s,e)=(s,e+(1,0)),\quad\nu(s,e)=(s,e+(0,1)),$$
with $X_0=X_R,\ X_1=X_L,\ Y_0=Y_T,\ Y_1=Y_B$.

> **(RD)** $(\Omega,\sigma,\tau)$ is (isomorphic to) the twisted diagonal of a reflection datum.
> **(RD4)** (RD) with every cycle-orbit of length 4. **(RD5)** (RD4) with no path-orbit of length $\equiv0\pmod4$. **(RD6)** (RD4) with all path-orbits of length $\le3$.
> **(RD7)** (RD4) with all path-orbits of length $\le2$ — equivalently $(XY)^2=1$ on every orbit of every corner type, i.e. $H^+$ is a quotient of the affine reflection group $D_\infty\times D_\infty$ of the grid.
