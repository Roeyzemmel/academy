## Statement

<!-- copied from writing/a4-candidate-generation.md §2.1, item W3; the draft stays the working copy -->

> **W3 (block-square test; argument given; Not settled, expected to clear).** Under (RD),
> hence under (A4), $[\sigma,\tau]$ lies in $G_0$ and preserves each (EP)-block $B_e$
> ($|B_e|=n/4$). There it equals $\rho_{XY}^{\pm2}$ (R2 Thm 3.1(d)), so **its restriction to
> every block is a square in $\mathrm{Sym}(B_e)$**. By the classical criterion for squares
> in $S_m$, this means that in each block each even cycle length occurs an even number of
> times. This refines R Cor. 1.7 blockwise. Unlike the global test, it is not forced by the
> sign, because a restriction to one block need not be even. In particular:
> - an odd cycle has length at most $n/4$;
> - an even cycle has length at most $n/8$ and has a partner of the same length in the same
>   block.
