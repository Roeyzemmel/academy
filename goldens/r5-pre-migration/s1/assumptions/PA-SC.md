## Definition

$P$ is simply connected. This is transverse to the chain: PA-6 ⇒ PA-SC, but PA-SC and PA-5w are **incomparable**, since Gauss–Bonnet permits one $4\pi$ corner on a disc. For why it is interesting, see GEO-4 (hyperellipticity) and OPEN-10.

witnesses (the 8-square fan and the $3\times3$ square frame) also show PA-SC and PA-5w
incomparable, previously resting on R §0.2 alone.
