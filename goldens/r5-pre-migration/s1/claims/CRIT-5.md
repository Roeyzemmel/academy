## Statement

> **Proposition 5.3 (Proved).** If some $u \in W$ acts as an $n$-cycle then
> $C^{\mathrm{pow}}$ is transitive, so (Q2) holds.
>
> **Geometric form.** The cycles of $w_{p,q}(\sigma,\tau)$ are the *levels* of the
> cylinders of $M$ in direction $(q,p)$: a cylinder of circumference $c$ and height
> $h$ contributes $h$ cycles of length $c$. So $u$ is an $n$-cycle exactly when the
> direction is **one-cylinder of height one**. Hence (Q2) holds for every origami
> possessing such a direction.
