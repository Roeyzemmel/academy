## Definition

OA-1 with some such $\iota$ satisfying $\iota^2=1$, i.e. an affine **involution** with derivative $-I$. It is not assumed hyperelliptic (GEO-4). Strictly stronger than OA-1 (GEO-1).

## Relations

OA-2 ⇒ OA-1: GEO-1.
