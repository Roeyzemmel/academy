## Statement

> **Proposition 5.4 (Proved).** A **normal** origami has a one-cylinder height-one
> direction **iff $G$ is cyclic**.
>
## Proof

> *Proof.* Such a direction forces $u$ to be an $n$-cycle. In the regular action
> every word acts by left multiplication by some $g \in G$, whose orbits are the
> right cosets of $\langle g\rangle$, giving cycle type $|g|^{\,n/|g|}$. So $u$ is
> an $n$-cycle iff $|u| = n$ iff $G = \langle u\rangle$. $\square$
