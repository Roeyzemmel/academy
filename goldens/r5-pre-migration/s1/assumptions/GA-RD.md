## Definition

$(\sigma,\tau)$ is the twisted diagonal of a reflection datum (DEF-2). The local conditions (RD4)–(RD7) mirror PA-4, PA-5w, PA-6 and PA-7; (RD7) is the flat (torus) case. (RD) ⇔ GA-EP ∧ (A3$^{\rm std}$) (STR-2).

## Relations

GA-RD ⇒ OA-3 ∧ GA-EP: STR-1. The chain (RD7)⇒(RD6)⇒(RD5)⇒(RD4)⇒(RD): R2 §1.3. The EW satisfies OA-3 ∧ GA-EP but not GA-RD (STR-2).
