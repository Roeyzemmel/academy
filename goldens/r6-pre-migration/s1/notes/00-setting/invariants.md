## [R2 §0.3] The three arithmetic conditions

> **(EP)** $\Lambda\subseteq2\mathbb Z^2$. Then $\mathrm{ab}_2:G\to(\mathbb Z/2)^2$ exists, $G_0:=\ker\mathrm{ab}_2$ has index 4, the map $\beta:\Omega\to(\mathbb Z/2)^2$, $i\mapsto d(i_0,i)\bmod2$, is $G$-equivariant, its fibres $B_e$ are blocks, and $\iota(i,j):=d(i,j)\bmod2=\beta(i)+\beta(j)$.
>
> **(D0)** some $u\in W$ of direction $(q,p)$ has a $\langle u\rangle$-orbit longer than the order of $(q,p)$ in $A$. *(Equivalently $R^{\mathrm{pow}}\cap D_0\ne\emptyset$; vacuous if $n=|A|$.)*
>
> **(2T)** every nonempty $D_\delta$ is a single $G$-orbital. *(Equivalently $\mathrm{rank}\,G=|A|+[\,n>|A|\,]$.)*
