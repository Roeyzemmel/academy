## Statement

With $M$, $G$, $W$ as in [Q1](Q1.md), let $C^{\mathrm{pow}}$ be the closure of $W$ under
$G$-conjugation and under powers, and $R^{\mathrm{pow}}=\{(i,ci):c\in C^{\mathrm{pow}}\}$.
**(Q2): is $R^{\mathrm{pow}}$ all ordered pairs of distinct squares?** Equivalently, by
definition: is $C^{\mathrm{pow}}$ transitive on ordered pairs? Every square corner is marked.
The definitions are verbatim in [notes/00-setting/questions.md](../notes/00-setting/questions.md).

## Geometric meaning

**By GEO-30 (R2 Thm 2.2, Proved modulo EMM Thm 2.1 and Smillie–Weiss):** for a non-torsion base
point $z_0$, the illumination relation on the fibre over $z_0$ equals $R^{\mathrm{pow}}$. So (Q2)
holds iff the $n$ points of one (equivalently every) non-torsion fibre illuminate one another.
EMM enters only in this identification, and "$R^{\mathrm{pow}}$ is everything iff
$C^{\mathrm{pow}}$ is transitive" is definitional. It fails at torsion base points (GEO-30, remarks).

## Status by level

- **OA-0 … OA-3: Disproved.** CEX-1 ($M_{12}$ members, 24 squares) and CEX-3/CEX-4 (16 squares). None of them is PA-4 (GEO-13, CEX-3).
- **PA-4 … PA-7: Not settled.** This is OPEN-1, the current focus.
- **PA-6∖PA-7: Reduced** to OPEN-3, OPEN-4, OPEN-5 and the imprimitive case. If OPEN-2 holds, CRIT-17 reduces it to GA-CT′.
- **PA-7: Proved** (trivially: $G$ abelian, CRIT-13(a)).
- Known positive cases: CRIT-3, CRIT-5, CRIT-9, CRIT-12, CRIT-15, and CRIT-17 wherever GA-2T′ holds.
