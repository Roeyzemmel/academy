## Statement

<!-- copied from writing/n8-generalization.md §2.3, item G6; the draft stays the working copy -->

> **G6 (argument complete; Not settled).** Let $A'=\ker\chi$ be non-cyclic.
> (a) $A'$ is a union of three cyclic subgroups iff $A'\cong(\mathbb Z/2)^2\times\mathbb Z/k$ with $k$ odd.
> **So every datum with $A'$ non-cyclic and not of this form fails (Q2)**. Examples are
> $A'\cong\mathbb Z/2\times\mathbb Z/4$, $(\mathbb Z/2)^3$ and $(\mathbb Z/3)^2$.
> (b) For $|A_p|=8$, the failing data are exactly those with $A_p\cong\mathbb Z/2\times\mathbb Z/4$ and
> $\chi(x,y)=y\bmod2$. **Every such datum fails.** These give 16-square counterexamples.
