## Statement

> **Proposition 2.2 (Proved).** (A3) leaves the parity obstruction of S1 §3.2
> untouched, because $\phi(a,b)$ acts trivially on $G/[G,G]G^2$ ($\sigma$ and
> $\sigma^{-1}$ have the same image mod squares). In particular **(A3), and a
> fortiori (A4)–(A6), does not rescue (Q1)**.

Crucially, (A3) does not enlarge $C$ by a single element. It changes the
*criterion*, not the *object*.
