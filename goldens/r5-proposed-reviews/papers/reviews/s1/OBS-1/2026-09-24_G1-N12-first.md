  used, exactly" misreports this.
- (c) UNJUSTIFIED: "β exists iff Λ⊆Λ′" has no derivation. It is two lines from R2 Def/Lemma
  0.1 and is not used by G1.
- (d) Wording: "not used: every sense of primitivity". The proof does use primitive vectors
  of ℤ², which is none of terminology.md's three senses.
- (e) "Sharper than W9a" holds as an implication; it was not seen to be strict on 7 normal
  origamis.

  statement.
