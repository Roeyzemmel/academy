## Statement

<!-- copied from writing/a4-candidate-generation.md §2.4, item W10; the draft stays the working copy -->

> **W10 (sketch; Not settled).** For a **non-normal** garage cover $P\to P'$ (degree at least
> 3, needing $\pi_1(P')$ of rank at least 2), pairs in a common fibre within one $K_4$-copy
> have $d=0$, because the holonomy of the projected loop is zero. Being in a common fibre is a
> $G$-invariant property of a pair. So if $D_0$ also contains a pair in two different fibres
> that is not in any $\mathrm{gr}(T)$, then **(2T′) fails**. That puts such covers in tier 1
> of axis 1 of the families.md ranking, the natural place R2 §4.5 points to.
>
> The hypothesis is checkable per example. It is expected to be typical, but that is not
> proved.
