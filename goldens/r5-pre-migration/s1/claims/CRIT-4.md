## Statement

> **Remark 5.2 (context, not a hypothesis).** Zmiaikou's stratum-wise dichotomy
> says a primitive origami in $\mathcal{H}(k_1,\dots,k_\sigma)$ tiled by
> $N \ge \big(2\sum_n(k_n+1)\big)^2$ squares has monodromy $\mathrm{Alt}$ or
> $\mathrm{Sym}$, hence 2-transitive, hence case (a); and below that threshold in
> the minimal stratum the Aougab–Friedman-Brown–Jeffreys–Ma list
> ($\mathrm{Alt}_{2g-1}$, $\mathrm{PSL}(d,q)$, $M_{11}$, $M_{23}$,
> $\mathrm{P\Gamma L}(2,8)$) is entirely 2-transitive. Together these suggest the
> residual class is small in practice. **They are not used as inputs**, for four
> reasons: the constant is unverified (S1 §7.1); which sense of "primitive" is
> meant is unverified (§0.1); the statement is a per-stratum threshold and *not* a
> density statement (Zmiaikou's own probabilistic paper shows the proportion
> generating $A_n/S_n$ tends to zero when the commutator is a fixed 3-cycle); and
> primitive below threshold outside the minimal stratum is covered by neither
> result, while primitivity in sense 1 does not imply 2-transitivity ($S_5$ on
> 2-subsets is primitive of rank 3).
