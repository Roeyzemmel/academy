## Statement

> **Proposition 4.3 (Proved).** For a **normal** origami the algebraic and
> geometric relations coincide. $G$ is then the deck group, acting on
> $\Omega = G$ by right multiplication; illumination-reachability is preserved by
> deck transformations, which send a difference $h = g^{-1}g'$ to $k^{-1}hk$, so
> the set of realised differences is conjugation-closed and equals the conjugation
> closure of the rotation values, namely $C^{\mathrm{pow}}$. $\square$
