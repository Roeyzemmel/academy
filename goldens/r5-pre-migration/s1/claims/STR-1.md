## Statement

**Theorem 3.1.** Let $(S;X_L,X_R,Y_B,Y_T)$ be a reflection datum and $(\Omega,\sigma,\tau,\mu,\nu)$
its twisted diagonal. Then:
(a) $G=\langle\sigma,\tau\rangle$ is transitive; $\mu,\nu$ realise **(A3)** ($\mu\sigma\mu=\sigma^{-1}$,
$\nu\tau\nu=\tau^{-1}$, $\mu\tau=\tau\mu$, $\nu\sigma=\sigma\nu$, $\Phi=\langle\mu,\nu\rangle\cong K_4$ free);
the blocks $B_e=S\times\{e\}$ are the fibres of $\beta$ and **(EP)** holds. So (RD) ⇒ (A3)∧(EP).
(b) $G^+=G^+_0\rtimes\Phi$, where $G^+_0$ (kernel of the action on blocks) is generated by
$$\sigma\mu=(X_R,X_L,X_R,X_L),\ \ \mu\sigma=(X_L,X_R,X_L,X_R),\ \ \tau\nu=(Y_T,Y_T,Y_B,Y_B),\ \ \nu\tau=(Y_B,Y_B,Y_T,Y_T)$$
(coordinates $e=(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)$): $G^+_0$ is the image of the twisted diagonal
$\Gamma\to(H^+)^4$, $w\mapsto(w,w^\mu,w^\nu,w^{\mu\nu})$ ($w^\mu$: swap $r_L\leftrightarrow r_R$;
$w^\nu$: swap $r_B\leftrightarrow r_T$). $G_0=G\cap G^+_0$ has index $[G^+:G]\in\{1,2,4\}$ in
$G^+_0$ and projects onto $H$ in every coordinate.
(c) $G\le H^+\wr(\mathbb Z/2)^2$; $|G|\le4|H^+|^4$; $G$ is imprimitive for $n>4$; the
composition factors of $G$ are those of $H^+$ and $\mathbb Z/2$.
(d) On the block $B_e$, $[\sigma,\tau]$ acts as $\rho_{XY}^{\pm2}$ for the corner type $XY$
determined by $e$ ($e=(0,0)$: $RT$). On a path-orbit of length $\ell$, $\rho_{XY}$ is an
$\ell$-cycle, so $\rho_{XY}^2$ is one $\ell$-cycle ($\ell$ odd) or two $\ell/2$-cycles ($\ell$
even); on a $4$-cycle-orbit $\rho_{XY}$ is a double transposition and $\rho_{XY}^2=1$. This is
R Prop. 1.6 in datum language. Consequently: **(RD5) ⇔ (RD4) and all cycles of $[\sigma,\tau]$
odd** (R Prop. 1.8); **(RD6) ⇒ $[\sigma,\tau]$ of type $3^k1^{n-3k}$**, $k$ the number of
path-orbits of length 3 (reflex vertices); **(RD7) ⇔ (RD4) and $[\sigma,\tau]=1$ ⇔ (RD4) and
$G$ abelian** (under (RD4) the orbits on which $\rho_{XY}^2=1$ are exactly the paths of
length $\le2$ and the $4$-cycles; $[\sigma,\tau]=1$ makes $M$ a torus and $G$ regular abelian;
conversely $G$ abelian kills the commutator). Without (RD4), $[\sigma,\tau]=1$ also allows
cycle-orbits of length 2 (orbifold points of angle $\pi$).
(e) *(Existence.)* A pair $(\sigma,\tau)$ arises under (RD4) [(RD5), (RD6)] iff it is the twisted
diagonal of a reflection datum whose dihedral orbits are paths and $4$-cycles [with the
stated length conditions]; the datum is recovered from $(\Omega,\sigma,\tau,\Phi)$ as
$S=B_{(0,0)}$ with $X_R=\sigma\mu|_S$, $X_L=\mu\sigma|_S$, $Y_T=\tau\nu|_S$, $Y_B=\nu\tau|_S$.

## Proof

*Proof.* (a) Relations: read off the formulas. Transitivity: $G^+\supseteq G^+_0$ acts on each
block through all of $H^+$ (every generator of $\Gamma$ is realised on $B_e$ by a generator
of $G^+_0$), so $G^+$ is transitive; $G^+=G\Phi$ with $\Phi$ acting only on the $e$-coordinate,
and a fixed point $s$ of $X_R$ gives $\sigma(s,e)=(s,e+(1,0))$, a fixed point of $Y$ gives the
other shift, so $G$ reaches every block from every point: transitive. (EP): the block shift
$G\to(\mathbb Z/2)^2$ is a homomorphism killing $G_i$, so $\mathrm{ab}(\mathrm{Stab}_F(i))\subseteq2\mathbb Z^2$.
(b) The four involutions lie in the block-kernel and are permuted by $\mu,\nu$-conjugation;
with $\Phi$ they generate $G^+$; $\Phi\cap$ kernel $=1$. (c) immediate. (d) Apply $[y,x]=yxy^{-1}x^{-1}$ letter by letter to
$(s,(0,0))$: $Y_T$; then $X_R$ (now $e=(0,1)$); then $Y_T$ (as $\tau^{-1}(s',e')=(Y_{e'_2+1}s',e'+(0,1))$);
then $X_R$: the result is $(X_RY_T)^2$ acting on $s$ with $e$ back to $(0,0)$. The other
blocks are transported by $\mu,\nu$, which swap $L\leftrightarrow R$, $B\leftrightarrow T$. (e) by
(a)–(b). $\square$
