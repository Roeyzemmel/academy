## Statement

> **Corollary 1.7 (necessary condition under (A4); Proved).** The number of cycles
> of $[\sigma,\tau]$ of each **even** length is **even**. Even lengths cannot come
> from the first row ($m$ would have to be odd) or the third (length 1), so they
> arise only from even-$m$ corners, two at a time. $\square$
