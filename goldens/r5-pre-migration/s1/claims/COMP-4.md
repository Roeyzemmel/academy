## Statement

### N5 (Not settled; a restatement, expected to clear immediately) — three necessary conditions in computational form

**N5.** The computational form of three necessary conditions this database has already
proved, recorded here so the pipeline can cite one item:

1. **(CT′)(i)** — for every non-identity $T\in Z=C_{S_n}(G)$, some value $u$ has a
   cyclic-subgroup orbit $O$ with $T|_O$ equal to a power of $u|_O$;
2. **(D0)** when $n>|A|$;
3. every $G$-orbital on unordered pairs is met.

*Sources.* R2 Thm 4.7(c); R2 Prop. 4.1(b),(c); R2 §8.1's list of necessary conditions.

*What would settle it.* Checking that each computational form is equivalent to the statement
in its source, with no strengthening.
