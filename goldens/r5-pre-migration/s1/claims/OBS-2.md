## Statement

### N13 (Proved) — the checkable test from $(\sigma,\tau)$

**N13.** Let $Q=V_4=\{\mathrm{id},\pi_r,\pi_u,\pi_r\pi_u\}$ act regularly on $V=\{0,1,2,3\}$,
$\pi_r=(01)(23)$, $\pi_u=(03)(12)$, $\pi_r\pi_u=(02)(13)$, with $\sigma=[c_\sigma,\pi_r]$,
$\tau=[c_\tau,\pi_u]$ over a finite abelian group $A$. Let $K=\ker\psi$ and
$L_K=\{c:[c,\mathrm{id}]\in G\}\le A^V$. For $g=[c,\pi]$ with $\pi\ne\mathrm{id}$,
$g^2=[h_g,\mathrm{id}]$ with $h_g=c+c\circ\pi$.

(a) $L_K$ is the additive span of the twelve vectors $v\circ\pi$, $v\in\{c_{\sigma^2},c_{\tau^2},c_{(\sigma\tau)^2}\}$,
$\pi\in V_4$. $G$ is transitive iff the entries of these three vectors generate $A$.
(b) $\{h_g:g\in G\setminus K\}=\bigcup_{u\in\{\sigma,\tau,\sigma\tau\}}\big(c_{u^2}+(1+\psi(u))L_K\big)$,
$(1+\pi)\ell:=\ell+\ell\circ\pi$. Call the set of all entries of these vectors $S\subseteq A$.
(c) For $0\ne t\in A$: if $t\notin\langle s\rangle$ for every $s\in S$, then
$\mathrm{gr}(T_t)\cap R^{\mathrm{pow}}=\varnothing$ and (Q2) fails. $V$ is identified with
$\mathbb Z^2/2\mathbb Z^2$ by $0\mapsto0$, $1\mapsto\bar e_1$, $3\mapsto\bar e_2$,
$2\mapsto\bar e_1+\bar e_2$, under which $\pi_r,\pi_u$ are translation by $\bar e_1,\bar e_2$,
so $\beta(m,j)=j$ is a level-$2\mathbb Z^2$ fibration in the sense of N12.
(d) (Partial converse.) If $0\ne t$ and $t\in\langle c_{u^2}(j)\rangle$ for some
$u\in\{\sigma,\tau,\sigma\tau\}$ and some $j$, then $\mathrm{gr}(T_t)\subseteq R^{\mathrm{pow}}$.

## Proof

*Proof.* (a) $K$ is abelian; the quotient $G/\langle\!\langle\sigma^2,\tau^2,(\sigma\tau)^2\rangle\!\rangle$
is generated by two involutions whose product is an involution, so has order at most 4, and
maps onto $\psi(G)=V_4$; hence $K=\langle\!\langle\sigma^2,\tau^2,(\sigma\tau)^2\rangle\!\rangle$.
Conjugation gives $[d,\pi][\ell,\mathrm{id}][d,\pi]^{-1}=[\ell\circ\pi^{-1},\mathrm{id}]$, so the
normal closure is spanned by the $V_4$-images; $V_4$ regular on $V$ gives the coordinate-0
projection as generated by all entries. $G$ transitive iff $\psi(G)$ transitive on $V$ (it is)
and $K$ transitive on the block $\{j=0\}$. (b) The coset $\psi^{-1}(\psi(u))$ is
$\{[c_u+\ell,\psi(u)]:\ell\in L_K\}$, giving $h=c_{u^2}+(1+\psi(u))\ell$. (c) By (b),
$T_tx\in\langle g^2\rangle x$ forces $t\in\langle h_g(j)\rangle$, contradicting the hypothesis, so
(NP$_{T_t}$) holds; apply N12 with $\beta(m,j)=j$, $\Lambda'=2\mathbb Z^2$. (d) If
$t=l\,c_{u^2}(j)$, $u^{2l}(m,j)=T_t(m,j)$ with $u\in\{\sigma,\tau,\sigma\tau\}$ a Christoffel
value, so $(x,T_tx)\in R^{\mathrm{pow}}$ for one $x$, hence for the whole orbital
$\mathrm{gr}(T_t)$. $\square$
