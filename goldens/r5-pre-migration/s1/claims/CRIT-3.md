## Statement

> **Theorem 5.1 (Proved).** (Q2) holds in each of the following cases — all at
> level (A0), i.e. requiring no symmetry hypothesis at all.
>
> **(a) $G$ is 2-transitive.** Then there is a single non-diagonal orbital and
> already (Q1) holds (S1 §3.1), hence (Q2).
>
> **(b) $G$ is abelian.** Then $G$ is regular; writing additively, any $g \in G$
> is $a\sigma + b\tau$ for some $(a,b)\ne(0,0)$; with $d = \gcd(a,b)$ and
> $(p,q) = (a/d,b/d)$ coprime, $g = d\,(p\sigma+q\tau)$ is a power of a Christoffel
> value. $\square$
>
> **(c) Some $u \in W$ acts as an $n$-cycle** — see Proposition 5.3. Then
> $\langle u\rangle$ is already transitive.
>
> **(d) Normal origamis whose $G$ is covered by conjugates of the cyclic
> subgroups generated by Christoffel values** — see §5.3. Verified by hand for
> $Q_8$, $D_4$ and $\mathbb{Z}/4\times\mathbb{Z}/2$.

Each of (a)–(d) is a condition checkable directly on $(\sigma,\tau)$; none
requires a citation. **The residual class is therefore: $G$ not 2-transitive and
not abelian, with no $n$-cycle in $W$** — and, in the normal case, failing the
covering test of §5.3.
