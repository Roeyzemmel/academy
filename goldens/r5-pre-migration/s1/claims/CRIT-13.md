## Statement

**Corollary 4.3.** (a) $G$ abelian ⇒ $G\cong A$ regular, (2T), $D_0=\emptyset$, (Q2) (R Thm 5.1(b));
under (A6) this is exactly (A7), where (Q2) holds and (Q1) fails except for the unit square
(R Prop. 3.1).
(b) Under (EP), $n>4$: if the $G$-orbitals are exactly the four $\iota$-classes then
$\Lambda=2\mathbb Z^2$ and (Q2) holds with $K\le2$ ($\sigma,\tau,\sigma\tau$ and one of $\sigma^2,\tau^2$),
provided $\sigma,\tau,\sigma\tau$ are fixed-point-free and $\sigma^2$ or $\tau^2\ne1$ — automatic
under (RD) with $m>1$ (a fixed point of $\sigma\tau$ would need block shift $(1,1)=0$).
