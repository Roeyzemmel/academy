## [R §1.2] (A3): the two caveats

The derivatives realised are $\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)$,
$\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ and $-I$. The first two have determinant $-1$:

- **Orientation.** $\mu,\nu \notin \mathrm{Aff}^+(M)$. (A3) is a statement about
  the extended group $\mathrm{SL}^\pm(M)$, not the Veech group as usually defined.
- **Splitting.** The derivative map $\mathrm{Aff}^\pm(M)\to\mathrm{GL}(2,\mathbb{Z})$
  has the translations as kernel; a $K_4$ in the image need not lift to a $K_4$
  upstairs. Requiring $\phi$ to be a homomorphism is therefore strictly more than
  "$K_4 \le \mathrm{SL}^\pm(M)$". Under (A4) it splits, since the $K_4$ is the deck
  group of $M \to P$.
