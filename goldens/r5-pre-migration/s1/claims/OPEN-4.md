## Statement

**Open 5.B.** Characterise the fold-imprimitive polyominoes. The conjecture: $H^+$ is
imprimitive iff $P$ has a grid-line/lattice-point symmetry or $\Lambda\ne2\mathbb Z^2$. The
verbatim text of 5.B shares a paragraph with 5.A, so it lives in [OPEN-3](OPEN-3.md).

R2 §5.3 (Partial), verbatim:

By Prop. 3.3, $H^+$ is primitive iff $P$ is fold-primitive. Obstructions: grid-line
reflections and lattice-point half-turns (Prop. 3.3(b)); $\Lambda\ne2\mathbb Z^2$ (the fibres of
$\beta$ refined by $\Omega\to A$ are $H$-blocks); rectangles ($H^+=D_p\times D_q$ on rows$\times$columns).
