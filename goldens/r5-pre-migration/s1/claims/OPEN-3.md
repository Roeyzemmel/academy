## Statement

**Open 5.A** Isolate a $3$-cycle when every corner type has $\ge2$ reflex vertices
(commutators with generators do not shrink supports; candidates are products of
$\rho$'s of adjacent types). **Open 5.B** Characterise fold-imprimitive polyominoes;
conjecture: imprimitive iff a grid-line/lattice-point symmetry or $\Lambda\ne2\mathbb Z^2$.
