## Definition

$P$ is a $p\times q$ rectangle. This is the trivial level: $M=\mathbb R^2/(2p\mathbb Z\oplus2q\mathbb Z)$, $G=\mathbb Z/2p\times\mathbb Z/2q$ regular. R2 added it for completeness.

## Relations

Under PA-6: PA-7 ⇔ $G$ abelian ⇔ $G$ regular (R2 §1.3).
