## Statement

> **Corollary 1.10 (Proved).** Under (A6) the case $k=0$ is exactly the rectangle
> family of Proposition 3.1, where (Q1) fails for every rectangle but the unit
> square. So under (A6) "at least one reflex corner" is a meaningful additional
> hypothesis excising precisely those counterexamples; it is *not* meaningful under
> (A3) or (A4), which carry no such constraint. Whether (Q1) survives under (A6)
> with $k\ge1$ is Not settled; a few examples suffice for present purposes.
