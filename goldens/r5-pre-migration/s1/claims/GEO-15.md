## Statement

> **Corollary 1.8a (Proved, modulo Kontsevich–Zorich).** Under (A5) the parity of
> the spin structure of $M$ is defined, since that invariant exists exactly when
> all zero orders are even (reference sheet §4.2). (A5) is therefore precisely the
> hypothesis placing $M$ in a stratum carrying a spin invariant.
>
> ⚠ **Scope.** Proposition 1.8 is a criterion *within* (A4): it presupposes that
> $M$ is an unfolding of a garage at all. It is not a standalone test on an
> arbitrary $(\sigma,\tau)$ — for that one first needs the realisation problem of
> Remark 1.11.
