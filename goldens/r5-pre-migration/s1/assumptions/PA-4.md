## Definition

$M$ is the unfolding of a rectangle-tiled parking garage $P$. $P$ is immersed, has **no interior cone points** and has horizontal/vertical boundary segments; its corners may have any angle in $\tfrac\pi2\mathbb Z$. $P$ need not be simply connected, and $h$ need not be injective. Transfer between $P$ and $M$ only through GEO-9 (R Prop 1.6).
