## Statement

For the cyclic covers $M_N(a)$: does (Q2) fail **only if** $\gcd(a_i+a_j,N)\ne2$ for all six
pairs? OBS-5 proves the "if" direction. The "only if" direction is data-only for $N\le18$
(runs E2a, E2b).
