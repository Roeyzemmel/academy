## Statement

**Theorem 3.4.** Let $(\sigma,\tau)$ satisfy (RD), $n=4m$.
(a) *Quotient and parity.* $4\mid n$; (EP) holds; $G\to(\mathbb Z/2)^2$, $\sigma\mapsto(1,0)$,
$\tau\mapsto(0,1)$ is onto. So $G$ is not perfect and $G\ne A_n,S_n$.
(b) *Evenness.* $\sigma,\tau\in A_n$, hence $G\le A_n$. *(The $\sigma$-cycle through $(s,e)$
runs through the $\langle X_L,X_R\rangle$-orbit of $s$ with $e_1$ alternating: a path-orbit
of length $\ell$ gives one cycle of length $2\ell$ per value of $e_2$, a cycle-orbit of
length $2\ell'$ gives two cycles of length $2\ell'$ per $e_2$; so $\sigma$ has an even number
of cycles, all of even length.)*
(c) *Imprimitivity and rank.* For $n>4$, $G$ is imprimitive (blocks $B_e$), hence neither
primitive nor 2‑transitive, and $\mathrm{rank}\,G\ge5$ (four $\iota$-classes plus the
diagonal; $\{\iota=0\}\ne\emptyset$). No element of $G$ is an $n$-cycle (it would be
transitive on the four blocks through $(\mathbb Z/2)^2$). So R Thm 5.1(a), R Prop. 5.3 and
Zmiaikou's theorem never apply.
(d) *Wreath bound and generators.* $G\le H^+\wr K_4$, $|G|\le4|H^+|^4\le4(m!)^4$;
$G^+=G^+_0\rtimes K_4$ with $G^+_0$ generated by four involutions, i.e. $G^+$ is a
quotient of $\Gamma\rtimes K_4$ ($K_4$ permuting the four generators of $\Gamma$); the
composition factors of $G$ are among those of $H^+\le S_m$ and $\mathbb Z/2$.
(e) *(Q1) fails* for $n>4$ (Prop. 4.1(d)).
(f) *Commutator.* Under (RD4): cycles of $[\sigma,\tau]$ of each even length come in pairs
(R Cor. 1.7). Under (RD5): $[\sigma,\tau]$ has odd order. Under (RD6): $[\sigma,\tau]$ has type
$3^k1^{n-3k}$.
(g) *Under (A6):* $G$ abelian $\iff$ $G$ regular (normal origami) $\iff[\sigma,\tau]=1\iff$
(RD7) $\iff$ **(A7)**. *(Regular ⇒ every nonidentity element is fixed-point-free; $[\sigma,\tau]$
has $n-3k$ fixed points and $k\le m-1<n/3$, so $[\sigma,\tau]=1$ ⇒ $M$ is a torus ⇒ $k=0$ ⇒
rectangle by R Prop. 1.9; rectangle ⇒ abelian by R Prop. 3.1.)* Genus $g=k+1\le m=n/4$, with
$g=1$ exactly under (A7). So **(A7) is the trivial level**: everything about it is
R Prop. 3.1 and Cor. 4.3(a), and every non-abelian phenomenon of this note lives in
(A6)∖(A7).
(h) *Under (EP)∧(HA):* $|Z|\le4$ for $Z=C_{S_n}(G)$; the blocks $B_e,B_{e'}$ are *linked*
(the projection of $G_0$ to $\mathrm{Sym}(B_e)\times\mathrm{Sym}(B_{e'})$ is the graph of an
isomorphism) iff some $T\in Z$ has shift $e'-e$; $G_0\cap A_m^{\,4}\cong A_m^{\,4/|Z|}$ (one
diagonal per $Z$-coset), and
$$4\,(m!/2)^{4/|Z|}\ \le\ |G|\ \le\ 4\,(m!)^{4/|Z|}.$$
*(A subdirect product of copies of the simple group $A_m$ is a product of diagonals over
its linkage classes; the linkage classes are the $Z$-cosets by the proof of Thm 4.5(c);
$[G:G_0]=4$.)* Checked: U5 ($|Z|=2$): $4\cdot60^2=14400=|G|$; L‑tetromino ($|Z|=1$):
$4\cdot12^4\le165888\le4\cdot24^4$.
