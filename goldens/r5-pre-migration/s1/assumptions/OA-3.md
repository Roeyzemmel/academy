## Definition

An injective $\phi:K_4\hookrightarrow S_n$ with $\phi(a,b)\sigma\phi(a,b)^{-1}=\sigma^a$ and $\phi(a,b)\tau\phi(a,b)^{-1}=\tau^b$, acting **freely**. Write $\mu=\phi(-1,1)$, $\nu=\phi(1,-1)$, $\Phi=\phi(K_4)$, $G^+=G\Phi$. The variant with standard shifts, (A3$^{\rm std}$), is in STR-2. This is the weakest algebraic level, and it is what the structural results use.
