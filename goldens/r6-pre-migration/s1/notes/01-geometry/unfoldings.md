## [R2 §2.2] Unfoldings are twisted diagonals: (A4) ⇒ (RD4), (A5) ⇒ (RD5), (A6) ⇒ (RD6) (Proved)

Let $P$ be a square-tiled surface with boundary: a finite connected set $S$ of unit squares
with partial injections $r,u$ (right/up neighbour), glued by translations; $\mathrm{hol}(P)\subseteq\mathbb Z^2$
the holonomies of closed loops in the adjacency graph. R Prop. 1.5 defines $(\sigma,\tau)$ on
$S\times K_4$ for any such $P$ (no immersion used), with $\Lambda\supseteq\mathrm{hol}(P)$.
A *parity function* $p:S\to(\mathbb Z/2)^2$, $p(rs)=p(s)+(1,0)$, $p(us)=p(s)+(0,1)$, exists iff
$\mathrm{hol}(P)\subseteq2\mathbb Z^2$; given $p$ put
$X_R(s)=r^{(-1)^{p_1(s)}}(s)$, $X_L(s)=r^{-(-1)^{p_1(s)}}(s)$ (and $s$ where undefined),
$Y_T,Y_B$ likewise with $u,p_2$: the moves across the edges that the folding $P\to Q=[0,1]^2$
sends to the four sides of $Q$.
