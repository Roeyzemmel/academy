## Statement

> **Proposition 1.6 (Proved).** Unfolding by $D_2$, a boundary corner of $P$ of
> angle $\theta = \pi p/q$ (lowest terms) gives $2/q$ cone points of $M$ of angle
> $2\pi p$; an interior point of $P$ lies on no reflection axis, has four
> preimages, and by the immersion hypothesis is always regular. Since a cone point
> of $M$ of angle $2\pi\ell$ is a cycle of length $\ell$ in $[\sigma,\tau]$:
>
> | source in $P$ | cycles of $[\sigma,\tau]$ |
> |---|---|
> | corner of angle $m\pi/2$, $m$ **odd** | one cycle of length $m$ |
> | corner of angle $m\pi/2$, $m$ **even** | two cycles of length $m/2$ |
> | interior lattice point (angle $2\pi$) | four fixed points |
>
> (Convex corner $m=1$: one fixed point. Reflex corner $m=3$: one 3-cycle.
> Boundary-edge-interior lattice point $m=2$: two fixed points. Ramp corner
> $m=4$, angle $2\pi$: two cycles of length 2.)
