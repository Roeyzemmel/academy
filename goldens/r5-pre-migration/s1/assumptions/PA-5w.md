## Definition

PA-4, and no corner of $P$ has angle $2\pi,4\pi,6\pi,\dots$; slit tips are excluded. Writing angles as $m\pi/2$, this forbids exactly $4\mid m$, so $3\pi/2$, $5\pi/2$ and $3\pi$ are allowed. **This is the old (A5): every verbatim R/R2 statement about \"(A5)\" means this.** It is characterised by GEO-14 (R Prop 1.8).

## Relations

PA-5w ⇒ PA-4: by definition.
