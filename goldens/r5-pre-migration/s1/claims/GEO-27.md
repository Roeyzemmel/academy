## Statement

> **Proposition 4.2 (conjugation is canonical; Proved).** Transporting the base
> point along a loop $\gamma$ twists the identification $F_{z_0}\leftrightarrow\Omega$
> by $\pi(\gamma)$, so the cutting-sequence word read in the original labelling
> becomes $\gamma^{-1}w_r\gamma$. Ranging over $\gamma$ gives the full
> $G$-conjugacy class.

Hence the $G$-conjugacy closure in the definition of $C$ is exactly the passage
from based loops to **free homotopy classes**, the canonical setting for a closed
geodesic. In Roey's formulation: let
$\mathrm{Cyl}(\mathbb{T}^2) \subset \pi_0\big(L(\mathbb{T}^2\setminus\{0\})\big)$
be the free homotopy classes containing a straight representative and
$\widetilde{\mathrm{Cyl}} \subset F_2$ its full preimage; then
$\pi(\widetilde{\mathrm{Cyl}}^{\,\mathbb{Z}}) = C^{\mathrm{pow}}$, and **(Q2) asks
whether $\mathrm{Cyl}$ moves every square index to every other.**
