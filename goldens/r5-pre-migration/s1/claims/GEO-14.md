## Statement

> **Proposition 1.8 (what (A5) says; Proved).** Assume (A4) and write corner
> angles of $P$ as $m\pi/2$. A corner of angle $2\pi n$ is one with $m = 4n$, so
> (A5) forbids exactly the corners with $4 \mid m$. By Proposition 1.6 an odd-$m$
> corner gives one cycle of odd length $m$; an even-$m$ corner gives two cycles of
> length $m/2$, which is odd precisely when $m \equiv 2 \pmod 4$; and interior
> lattice points give fixed points. Since $P$ has no interior cone points these are
> all the sources. Hence, **within (A4)**,
> $$\text{(A5)} \iff \text{every cycle of } [\sigma,\tau] \text{ has odd length}
> \iff \text{every zero of } M \text{ has even order.} \qquad\square$$
