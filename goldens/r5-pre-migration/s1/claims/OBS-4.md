## Statement

### N15 (Proved) — the exact criterion on the translation orbitals

**N15.** For the recipe with data $(A_p,a,\chi)$ and $0\ne t\in A'=\ker\chi$ (the translation
$T_t:(i,\varepsilon)\mapsto(i+t,\varepsilon)$):
$$\mathrm{gr}(T_t)\cap R^{\mathrm{pow}}=\varnothing\iff t\notin\langle a_0+a_3\rangle\cup\langle a_2+a_3\rangle\cup\langle a_0+a_2\rangle .$$
The right-hand side is symmetric under $S_4$ (the complementary pair sums are negatives), and
invariant under $\mathrm{Aut}(A_p,\chi)$, in particular under units in the cyclic case.
**Hence (Q2) fails whenever $A'\ne\bigcup_{i<j}\langle a_i+a_j\rangle$.**

## Proof

*Proof.* $A'=\{\sum n_ia_i:\sum n_i\text{ even}\}$ is generated by the pair sums and the $2a_j$
when the $a_j$ generate $A_p$ (e.g. $2a_0=s_1+s_3-s_2$, $2a_1=-(s_1+s_2+s_3)$, every pair sum
$\pm s_i$), so $\langle s_1,s_2,s_3\rangle=A'$; these are the entries of $c_{\sigma^2},c_{\tau^2},c_{(\sigma\tau)^2}$
(N14), so $G$ is transitive by N13(a). ($\Leftarrow$) N13(c): $\langle\pm(2k+1)s_i\rangle\subseteq\langle s_i\rangle$.
($\Rightarrow$) N13(d) with $u=\sigma,\tau,\sigma\tau$ and $j$ chosen where $e_i(j)=+1$. $\square$

*Gloss, not part of N15.* The reading of the pair sums $a_i+a_j$ as pillowcase monodromies
around pairs of corners, and of $\sigma^2,\tau^2,(\sigma\tau)^2$ as lifts of the horizontal,
vertical and diagonal pillowcase cylinders (so that N15 is N8 lead (a) with an argument),
rests on identifying `CyclicCover`'s labelling with FMZ's $\mathbb Z/N$ action — which the N8
proof lists as open. N15 itself does not use this identification; it is proved from the
transcribed labelling of N14 alone. **Update, 2026-09-24:** N20 gives this gloss an
independent argument for the two N8 members specifically, computing the corner monodromies
$d_v$ directly from the stored permutations without appeal to the FMZ identification. For
the rest of the cyclic-cover family the gloss still rests on the FMZ identification, or on
checking N19's hypotheses member by member as N20 does here; it is not discharged in
general.
