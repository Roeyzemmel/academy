## Statement

**Conjecture 10.1 ((2T′) under (A6)).** Every polyomino unfolding satisfies (2T′): its
$G$-orbitals are exactly the level sets of $\mathrm{inv}=(d,z)$. Evidence: all ten examples
of §8.2 (including the fold-imprimitive $2\times2$-block L and the three symmetric shapes);
proved under (HA) (Thm 4.7(a)). *What a counterexample must look like:* an intermediate
origami $M\to M'\to\mathbb T^2$ that is neither a torus quotient nor $M/T$ for translations
$T$ — under (A6), by the Galois correspondence, a polyomino $P$ folding non-normally onto a
smaller reflection surface $P'$ with $\Lambda(M)=2\mathbb Z^2$ and no grid-line symmetry (the
$2\times2$-block L folds non-normally but has $\Lambda=4\mathbb Z^2$, which explains its
orbitals). If Conjecture 10.1 holds, then by Thm 4.7 **(Q2) under (A6) is equivalent to
(CT′)**, i.e. to a statement about $T$-invariant Christoffel orbits for the centralising
elements only.
