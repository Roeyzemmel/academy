## Statement

<!-- copied from writing/a4-candidate-generation.md §2.3, item W2′; the draft stays the working copy -->

> **W2′ (elementary; Not settled).** If $[\sigma,\tau]$ has a fixed point, then every
> **normal** origami that $M$ covers is a torus. Such a quotient is regular, the fixed point
> maps to a fixed point, so its commutator is trivial.
>
> Hence **the maximal normal quotient of an (A4) unfolding is its maximal torus quotient
> $E_\Lambda$**, where (Q2) holds (R Thm 5.1(b)).
