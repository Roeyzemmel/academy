## Definition

There is $\iota\in S_n$ with $\iota\sigma\iota^{-1}=\sigma^{-1}$, $\iota\tau\iota^{-1}=\tau^{-1}$. Equivalently, $M$ has an affine automorphism with derivative $-I$ (GEO-2).
