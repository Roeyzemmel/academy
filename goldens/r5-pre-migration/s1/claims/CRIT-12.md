## Statement

**Theorem 4.2.** (2T), together with (D0) when $n>|A|$, implies (Q2), with an explicit
direction per class (any representative of $\delta\ne0$; the $u$ of (D0) raised to the order
of its direction for $\delta=0$). *(Prop. 4.1 and $G$-saturation.)*
