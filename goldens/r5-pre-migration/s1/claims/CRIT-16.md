## Statement

*Remark 4.6.* Under (HA) the intermediate-cover philosophy is complete: the only refinements
of the $\iota$-classes are the graphs of centralising involutions, i.e. $M\to M/T$. Under
(A6), by Prop. 2.4(b) and Thm 4.5(b), $C_{S_n}(G)=\{T_\theta\}$ for the symmetries $\theta$ of $P$ with
linear part in $K_4$ and half-integer axis/centre — at most three.
