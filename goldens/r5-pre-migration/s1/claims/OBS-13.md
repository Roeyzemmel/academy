> **M7 (lemma on derivative-$(-I)$ targets; argument given; Not settled).** Let $f\in\mathrm{Aff}(M)$ with $Df=-I$,
> $x\in M\setminus\Sigma^*$ and $fx\ne x$. Then $x$ illuminates $fx$ **iff** $f^2=1$ and $x$ illuminates some regular
> fixed point $p\in\mathrm{Fix}(f)\setminus\Sigma^*$.
