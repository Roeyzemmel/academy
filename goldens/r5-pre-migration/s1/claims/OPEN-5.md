## Statement

**Open 6.A (half-turns).** For $\lambda=-1$ no general direction is known. Data: S‑tetromino
(half-turn about $(\frac32,1)$, $\iota=(1,0)$): $\sigma\tau$ has an even number of orbits, none
invariant; $(\pm1,\pm2)$ work. Plus‑pentomino (half-turn about $(\frac32,\frac32)$, $\iota=(1,1)$):
met at $K=2$. Conjecture: some direction with $|p|+|q|\le3$ always works; Prop. 4.4(c) is the
available sufficient condition.
