## Statement

> **Remark 3.2.** The failure is a bookkeeping artefact rather than a geometric
> obstruction. On that torus, for generic $x$ in square $0$ and $y = x+(2,0)$, the
> segment between them has direction $(2+4a,2b)$ and midpoint $x+(1+2a,b)$, an
> integer translate of $x$ and hence not a marked point. So $x$ **does** illuminate
> $y$; the trajectory is $\sigma^2$, a horizontal path of length two. What (Q1)
> forbids is not the trajectory but the demand that the travel time be exactly one
> period. This is the argument that (Q2), not (Q1), is the geometrically correct
> formulation.
