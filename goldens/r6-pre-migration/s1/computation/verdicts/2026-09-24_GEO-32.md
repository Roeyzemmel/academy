GEO-32 findings, both runs:
- **Missing witness for PA-SC ⇏ PA-5 (both runs, UNJUSTIFIED → ADD_DERIVATION).** Gauss–Bonnet
  is only a necessary condition. The witness both runs found independently is the
  **8-square fan**: squares $Q_1,\dots,Q_8$ around one vertex $v$, with $Q_j$ in quadrant
  $j \bmod 4$ and consecutive squares glued along their edge from $v$, except $Q_8|Q_1$. Run A
  describes it as the two-sheeted $[-1,1]^2$ branched at 0 and cut along $[0,1]\times\{0\}$. It
  has $\chi=1$, ten corners of angle $\pi/2$, one of $4\pi$ ($m=8$), seven boundary points of
  angle $\pi$, and no interior cone points; Gauss–Bonnet gives $10\cdot\pi/2-3\pi=2\pi$. Its
  unfolding has 32 squares, genus 4, $\mathcal H(3,3)$. A smaller witness: the $2\times2$ square
  slit from its centre to a side midpoint, with one $2\pi$ corner, $\chi=1$, unfolding in
  $\mathcal H(1,1)$. Angles $\ge2\pi$ are allowed at PA-4: CW12 `parking.tex` l. 503–505, "no
  apriori upper bound on the angle at a vertex" (run A).
- **PA-5 ⇏ PA-SC (both runs).** The $3\times3$ square frame (embedded, not simple) has corners
  $\{\pi/2{:}4,\ 3\pi/2{:}4\}$ and $\chi=0$.
- **Garage or origami (both runs, ambiguity → state the reading).** Both runs prove the
  garage-level reading. For the origami-level reading ("M is the unfolding of some P with …"):
  - (a) no garage structure on the fan's $M$ is PA-5, because $[\sigma,\tau]$ has two 4-cycles
    and by GEO-9 (Proved) a PA-5 garage produces only odd cycles. This is by hand, in both runs.
  - (b) no garage structure on the frame's $M$ is simply connected. Both runs enumerate the
    free $K_4$-structures (run A: 16 commuting pairs, one garage; run B: 9 structures, one
    garage). Both also give a hyperellipticity route: a simply connected $P$ forces $\mu\nu$ to
    have $2g+2=12$ fixed points, but the derivative-$(-I)$ involutions have 8, 0, 8 and 8.
    Run A derived these counts by hand from the frame's symmetries (only $|C|=4$ came from the
    script). Run B notes that under the repo's rule its computational route alone would give at
    most "modulo".
- **Understated (run B).** The 6-square fan has a $3\pi$ corner and satisfies PA-4, PA-SC and
  PA-5w but not PA-5. So PA-SC does not imply PA-5 even under PA-5w. The same examples also
  show PA-SC and PA-5w incomparable (run A F4), which until now rested on R §0.2 alone, with
  the same Gauss–Bonnet gap.
- **Drift.** "square-frame polyomino" in the summary clashes with PA-6's "polyomino" = simple;
  write "the $3\times3$ square frame (embedded, not simple)". `assumptions/PA-5.md`'s summary
  still says "turning point". `CLAUDE.md` still says "PA-6 ⇒ PA-5 is pending (GEO-31)".
- **Leads, not findings (route separately).**
  - OPEN-10: for any PA-4 garage, the fixed points of $\mu\nu$ are exactly the lifts of the odd
    corners, and $\chi(M)=4\chi(P)-\#\mathrm{odd}$. So $\mu\nu$ has $2g+2$ fixed points iff
    $\chi(P)=1$; equivalently $M/\langle\mu\nu\rangle$ is the double of $P$. That would answer
    OPEN-10 affirmatively for $\iota=\mu\nu$. Both runs observed this; neither verified it as
    an item.
  - GEO-19 (N3), run A: its test is not sufficient. On the frame's $M$, two $K_4$-structures
    pass it, but their quotient has interior holonomy $-I$, so it is not a garage; two more
    flip 8 edges under $\mu\nu$.
