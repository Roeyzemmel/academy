## Statement

> **Corollary 5.5 (Proved).** The monodromy group is an $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{Z})$-orbit
> invariant (the action changes the basis of $F_2$ and preserves $\operatorname{im}\pi$),
> so every surface in a normal origami's orbit is normal with the same $G$.
> Therefore **the $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{Z})$-orbit of a normal origami with
> non-cyclic deck group contains no single-cylinder representative.**

This applies to all three running examples and shows the unrestricted
"every orbit has a one-cylinder representative" statement is **false**.
