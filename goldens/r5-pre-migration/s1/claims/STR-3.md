## Statement

**Lemma 3.2 (Proved).** (a) The $G$-orbitals inside $\{\iota=0\}$ correspond to the off-diagonal
$H$-orbitals on $B_e$; those inside $\{\iota=\epsilon\}$, $\epsilon\ne0$, to the orbits of $G_0$ on
$B_e\times B_{e+\epsilon}$ (a subdirect product of two conjugates of $H$).
(b) If $H^+\supseteq A_m$, $m\ge5$, then (HA) ($H\trianglelefteq H^+$ of index $\le4$, $A_m$ simple).
