## Statement

Let $M\to\mathbb T^2$ be an origami with monodromy $\sigma,\tau$ generating a transitive
$G\le S_n$, $W$ the Christoffel values in all four sign quadrants, and $C$ the closure of $W$
under $G$-conjugation. **(Q1): is $C$ transitive on ordered pairs of distinct squares?**
The definitions are verbatim in [notes/00-setting/questions.md](../notes/00-setting/questions.md).

## Geometric meaning

Without powers, $C$ records only trajectories that close up after one period. The
illumination question is (Q2), with powers. See [Q2](Q2.md) and GEO-30.

## Notes

- Fails already on rectangles, by a parity obstruction (CRIT-1 = R Prop 3.1). OA-3 does not rescue it (GEO-6 = R Prop 2.2).
- Open and secondary: (Q1) under PA-6 with $k\ge1$ reflex corners (OPEN-12).
