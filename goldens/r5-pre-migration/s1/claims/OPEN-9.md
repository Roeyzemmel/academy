## Statement

(a) Is the mechanism of BOUND-4 realised at $n=18$ ($p=3$, $|Q|=9$, $f=2$)? This is spec S5.
(b) Is $16$ the minimum number of squares of **any** (Q2) counterexample, not only one produced by
this mechanism? This is spec S2, over all origamis with $n\le15$.
